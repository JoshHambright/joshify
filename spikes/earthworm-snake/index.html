Field note. The pale swollen band a third of the way down the body is the clitellum; it secretes the cocoon and is the only reliable landmark on a worm. The dark granules dropped behind you are castings. Every cell you tunnel through stays loosened for the rest of the run — that aeration is what the animal is actually for.
