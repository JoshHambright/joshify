DENSITY sets how many pulses the Euclidean generator distributes across the 16 steps. CHAOS is the per-step chance the engine ignores the pattern — a dropped hit, a ghost note, an octave jump. SWING lengthens the downbeats and shortens the offbeats by the same amount, so the bar stays in time while the groove drags.

DELAY FEEDBACK NETWORK

The delay line is re-crushed on every pass, so repeats decay in resolution as well as amplitude. WOW is a modulated delay line — real pitch drift, not vibrato — so it detunes the repeats against each other.

GENERATOR MODE
STOCK MEDIA & PALETTE
MOTION FLOW FIELD & LONG-FORM DRIFT

STOCK swaps the four-colour grade the whole image is mapped through; DMG and AMBER also quantise to four tones. FADE lifts the blacks and washes the colour — the single biggest difference between "broken digital" and "old".

FLOW advects the previous frame along three drifting vortices instead of scaling it toward the centre, which is what makes the motion curl rather than pulse. BREATH is how far the feedback swings between contracting and blooming. EVOLVE is the rate of the long-form drifts — these are random walks, not cycles, so the scene never returns to a state you have already seen. At zero the whole structure holds still. RENDER is the internal buffer resolution; drop it first if the frame rate sags.

WET SLIDE OIL, WATER, DYE

The dyes are transparent, so overlapping blobs multiply rather than add — two colours cross to a deeper third instead of washing toward white. The bright line at every boundary is the caustic where the oil-water interface refracts the lamp. VISCOSITY is the merge threshold: low pools the blobs into one mass, high keeps them separate and tightens the rims.

DYE SURVIVAL is how much of the image's own colour lives through the film stock's grade. At zero you get the stock's monochrome transfer; at full, the dye and lamp colours come through whatever stock is loaded. The four-tone stocks ignore it — they are monochrome by design.
